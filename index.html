STEP 1

Research & Physical Activities

Exploring different types of exercise, health benefits, equipment needed, and safety guidelines for teenagers.

Endurance

Aerobic Exercise

Jogging, cycling, and swimming. Strengthens the cardiovascular system, improves stamina, and oxygenates the body.

Target: 3-4 times a week, 30 minutes each.
Muscle & Bone

Strength Training

Push-ups, pull-ups, and light dumbbells. Essential for building muscle strength, posture, and bone density.

Target: 2-3 sessions weekly with proper form.
Mobility

Flexibility & Yoga

Stretching exercises and yoga routines. Improves joint range of motion, muscle elasticity, and prevents injuries.

Target: Daily stretching before and after workouts.
Agility

Balance & Coordination

Single-leg stances, agility ladders, and balance boards. Enhances athletic performance and body awareness.

Target: 2 sessions weekly for 15-20 minutes.
Social & Outdoor

Outdoor Activities & Team Sports

Football, basketball, and volleyball. Combines intense aerobic exercise with teamwork, social bonding, and mental refreshment in fresh air.

Target: Weekend recreational games with friends.

Health Benefits

  • Boosts cardiovascular health and endurance
  • Releases endorphins to relieve stress and anxiety
  • Improves sleep quality, memory, and concentration
  • Strengthens the immune system and bones

Possible Risks

  • Overtraining without sufficient rest days
  • Muscle strains caused by skipping warm-up exercises
  • Injuries from improper form or unsupportive footwear

Equipment Needed

  • Supportive athletic running shoes
  • Reusable water bottle (1.5 - 2L)
  • Comfortable exercise/yoga mat
  • Breathable sportswear clothing

Jogging & Warm-up

Goal: Improve endurance

Yoga & Stretching

Flexibility routine focusing on posture and deep breathing.

Warm-up: 3 min gentle spine mobility
Cool-down: Relaxation & mindfulness
Goal: Increase flexibility

Strength Training

Bodyweight exercises: push-ups, squats, and core plank routine.

Warm-up: 5 min shoulder & arm circles
Cool-down: Upper & lower body stretch
Goal: Build muscular strength
Thursday 30 min

Active Rest / Light Walk

Low-intensity outdoor walking or casual cycling in fresh air.

Warm-up: Not required
Cool-down: Light hamstring stretch
Goal: Active recovery
Friday 30 min

Aerobic Cardio & Jump Rope

High-energy jump rope intervals mixed with jumping jacks.

Warm-up: 5 min dynamic warmup
Cool-down: Calf and hamstring stretching
Goal: Cardiovascular fitness

Warm-up: Pre-game light jog and drills

Full Rest Day

STEP 3

Healthy Lifestyle Advice for Teens

Essential recommendations covering nutrition, hydration, sleep hygiene, and mental well-being.

01. NUTRITION

Healthy Eating

Eat nutrient-rich meals daily containing fresh fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins.

02. HYDRATION

Drinking Water

Drink at least 1.5–2 liters of clean water daily to stay hydrated and boost focus.

03. SLEEP HYGIENE

Sleeping Habits

Maintain a consistent sleep schedule and aim for 8–9 hours of sleep every night.

04. DIGITAL DETOX

Reducing Screen Time

Limit recreational screen time on smartphones and computers to under 2 hours daily.

05. MENTAL WELLNESS

Managing Stress

Practice deep breathing exercises, mindfulness, or hobbies to reduce academic anxiety.

06. MOTIVATION

Staying Motivated

Work out with a buddy, log your milestones, and celebrate every small achievement.

07. HEALTHY CHOICES

Avoiding Junk Food

Replace sugary sodas and ultra-processed snacks with water, nuts, and fresh fruits.

08. BODY CARE

Correct Posture

Keep your spine straight while studying or using mobile devices to protect joint health.
